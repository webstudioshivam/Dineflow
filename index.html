Questions

Do guests need to install an app?

No. They scan the QR code with the phone camera and the menu opens in the browser.

How do I get paid?

Guests pay your UPI ID directly from their phone. Your staff then mark the order as paid on the kitchen screen. CaffCrew doesn't touch the money.

Can my staff have their own logins?

Yes. Invite kitchen, waiter and manager staff by email. A cook sees only the kitchen screen.

Is my data separate from other restaurants?

Yes. Each restaurant's menu, orders and sales are private to that restaurant.

What happens if I stop paying?

Guests can no longer place new orders. Renew and your restaurant goes live again with everything as you left it.

Do I need a computer?

No. Everything works on a phone. A tablet in the kitchen works well for the order screen.
